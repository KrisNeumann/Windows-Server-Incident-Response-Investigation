# Privacy / Breach Assessment (lab scenario)
- Data exposed: 920 payload bytes of personal-data-style records sent in clear text over raw TCP. Field types visible in the stream: name, sex, street address, email address, phone number, and identifier-formatted numbers. The values are fictitious lab data.
- Data was synthetic lab data; no real personal information.
- Scenario impact: the exposed records belong to the client's customers, who reported phishing and vishing. That is consistent with misuse of the exposed fields (contact details plus identifiers), though the capture alone does not prove it.
- Scenario recommendations: notify the client promptly with scope, affected fields, and timeline; advise affected customers to watch for phishing/vishing and, in a real case, offer credit or identity monitoring where the data warrants it.
- In a real environment: determine whether exposed data includes personal data; apply the applicable state/sector notification rules with counsel; record the decision and rationale. Not a legal determination.
